Subtitles are a crutch, and crutches are useful — as long as you eventually put
them down.

## The three modes

**Translated subtitles** — you understand the story but train almost nothing. Use
them only for a first pass on genuinely difficult material.

**Native subtitles** — you connect sound to spelling. This is where most of the
learning happens, and where you should spend the majority of your time.

**No subtitles** — the real test. Uncomfortable at first, and the fastest way to
train pure listening.

## A rotation that works

Watch an episode with native subtitles. Rewatch the best scene without any. If
you catch 80% of it, you are ready to move the whole series to subtitle-free.

## Mine, do not memorise

Do not try to learn every unknown word. Pick the ones that appear twice, or that
you can imagine yourself saying. Those are the ones worth a card.
