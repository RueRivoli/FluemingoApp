English is everywhere, which is both the good news and the bad news: it is easy
to consume a lot of it passively and learn very little. Here is how to make your
watching count.

## Choose by accent, not by hype

Pick a series whose accent matches the English you actually need. Business
English in London and casual English in California are not the same target.

## Our picks

### Friends

Still the classic for a reason: everyday vocabulary, short scenes, and a laugh
track that literally marks where the jokes are.

### The Good Place

Clear American English, philosophical vocabulary, and episodes short enough to
rewatch without pain.

### Sherlock

For advanced learners. Fast British English, rich vocabulary, and enough
subtitles-worthy monologues to keep you humble.

## The one habit that matters

Rewatch. A scene you have already understood is where fluency is built — the
second time through, your brain stops decoding and starts absorbing.
